Who We Are

We Fish These Waters

Every rod we build is spec'd by anglers who fish the same waters you do — Biscayne Bay, the Keys backcountry, the Intracoastal, the bridges and passes of Broward and Palm Beach.

We got tired of buying production rods that didn't quite fit. The handle was too long, the action too soft, the guides not rated for 65lb braid. So we started building our own — and then building for everyone else.

Every SFSW rod is a 1-piece, hand-wrapped build. We don't stock finished rods. We don't mass produce. When you order from us, a real person builds your rod by hand.

Build Process

How Every Rod Gets Made

Blank Prep

We inspect and prep every blank before it gets touched — checking for straightness, weight, and spine alignment.

Guide Spacing

Guides are spaced using a combination of manufacturer charts and our own inshore-tuned measurements for optimal line flow.

Hand Wrapping

Every guide is hand-wrapped in your chosen thread and trim colors using a powered wrapper for consistent tension.

Epoxy Finish

Two-part epoxy finish, applied and hand-rotated to prevent sagging. Cured 48 hours before shipping.
