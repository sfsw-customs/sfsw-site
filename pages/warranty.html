Warranty Details

Guides

Shit happens, but we stand behind our craftsmanship for life. We cover the labor on all guide repairs for the lifetime of the rod. Should a guide become physically damaged or broken, the replacement component will simply be billed at its current material cost.

Blanks

First off — our labor is free for life. If a blank breaks, we will never charge you a dime in custom labor to build the replacement rod. We will strip what we can, prep the new stick, and completely re-wrap and epoxy your setup on us.

Custom rod blanks carry individual warranties determined entirely by their manufacturer (like Rainshadow, Calstar, or United Composites).

  • Factory Defects: If the manufacturer deems the break a true material defect, they replace the raw blank for free.
  • Accidents & Abuse: If you stepped on it, slammed it, or high-sticked it, most premium manufacturers offer a "No-Fault" replacement program.

In either scenario, you are only responsible for the direct manufacturer processing fees, the raw cost of any components that couldn't be salvaged (like a destroyed guide or reel seat), and any necessary shipping costs to get the new blank to our bench.

** Components permanently epoxied to the broken blank — such as custom foam/cork grips, reel seats, and the thread art under-wraps — cannot be salvaged and will need to be replaced at current component costs.

** This warranty is non-transferable and applies strictly to the original owner with proof of purchase from our shop.
