Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: November 2026

South Florida Saltwater ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website sfsaltwater.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site or place an order, how we use it, and the limited circumstances under which it may be shared. We are a small, owner-operated custom rod building shop based in Boca Raton, Florida — we have no interest in selling your data or spamming your inbox.

Section 1
Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly to us, plus basic, anonymous site-usage statistics. This includes:

  • Order information: your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you place an order through our checkout.
  • Build specifications: the rod configuration options you select (blank, guides, colors, grips, etc.) so we can build exactly what you ordered.
  • Contact and warranty form submissions: your name, phone number, email, and any message you choose to send us through our contact or warranty claim forms.
  • Payment data: we do not store your credit card number, CVV, or full payment details. All payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe, Inc. Only a tokenized reference to your transaction is retained on our end.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: when you browse the site, we record which pages are viewed, the website that referred you (for example, Google or Instagram), your general device type (phone, tablet or computer), how long you spend on each page, the options you try in our custom rod builders, and actions such as adding a rod to your cart or starting checkout. To tell repeat visits apart, your browser stores a random ID in its local storage. This ID is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal details, and we do not record your IP address or location.

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or ad networks. Our usage statistics are stored in our own database and are used only to understand how the site is used and improve it. You can clear the stored ID at any time by clearing your browser's site data for sfsaltwater.com.

Section 2
How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to run our business and fulfill your order. Specifically:

  • To process and build your custom rod order
  • To contact you regarding your order status, build confirmations, or spec approvals
  • To arrange shipping or local delivery
  • To respond to warranty claims or customer service inquiries
  • To comply with legal obligations if required by law

We do not use your information for advertising, retargeting, or marketing lists. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own commercial purposes.

Section 3
Third-Party Services

To operate our website and process orders, we use a small number of trusted third-party services. Each handles your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe, Inc.: Processes all payment transactions. Your payment data is transmitted directly to and stored securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Google Firebase / Firestore: We store order records (your name, contact details, rod specs, and order total) in Google's Firebase cloud database to manage and fulfill your order. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
Section 4
Data Retention

We retain your order information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the order, honor our warranty commitments, and comply with any applicable legal, tax, or accounting requirements. If you would like us to delete your personal information from our records, please contact us directly and we will do so to the extent we are not legally required to retain it.

Section 5
Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Our website is served over HTTPS and your payment data is handled by Stripe, which maintains PCI DSS compliance. That said, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have any concerns about the security of your information, please contact us directly.

Section 6
Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. Because we are a small, direct business, the easiest way to exercise any of these rights is simply to contact us — we'll take care of it directly.

If you are a Florida resident, you may have additional rights under the Florida Digital Bill of Rights (FDBR) regarding your personal data. We do not sell personal data and do not engage in targeted advertising, so most FDBR provisions are not triggered by our activities, but you are welcome to contact us with any questions.

Section 7
Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our site, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Section 8
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
