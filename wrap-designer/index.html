Rounded to 0.5 mm

Every mark rounded to the nearest half millimeter for marking with a ruler. The gaps only get smaller toward the thin end, and every mark stays within 0.5 mm of its exact spot (usually within 0.25 mm). On long runs of small gaps, each mark is rounded on its own instead.
